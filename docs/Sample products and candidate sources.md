  Paid tiers:   
* Starter is \$25/month for 1,000 searches (200 per hour).   
* Production is \$150/month for 15,000 searches  
* Big Data is \$275/month for 30,000 searches

[ToS](https://serpapi.com/legal?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=1061187028&gbraid=0AAAAADD8kqNGQhMWmMEmbU0cb7QUhVs-U&gclid=Cj0KCQiA-8XXBhDaARIsADnEOrDYzOBYGk032RDCEueWv7bYofYyDsENE2y2ebEyYDujZ3a6Wx-gMo0aAlWuEALw_wcB):  
ToS: Storing/caching: 

* Terms never say whether you can save results in your own database, for how long, or whether you must refresh them.   
* Closest clause (Section 2): you agree not to reproduce, duplicate, copy, sell, resell, or exploit any portion of the Service or access to it without written permission.   
* Section 13 (U.S. Legal Shield): SerpApi takes on liability for lawfully collecting public search data, but not for how you use it. Uses it lists as illegal include copyright infringement and other IP violations.  
* SerpApi’s own retention: they keep your search data for 31 days, then delete it.

ToS: display/attribution

* No attribution requirement.   
* Section 22: SerpApi may use your company name and logo in case studies and marketing unless you ask them not to, and you get the same right in return. 


  




* Ebay  (Browse API)

Type: retailer/marketplace API  
Free tier: 

* Sign up: Joining the Developers Program provides a keyset  
* The Browse API’s default limit is 5,000 calls per day  
* Higher limits require an Application Growth Check.   
* Unused calls don’t roll over to the next period

	[ToS](https://developer.ebay.com/join/api-license-agreement):  
	ToS: storing/caching 

* You may make limited intermediate copies of eBay Content only as needed, and must delete them when no longer required  
* Displayed item listing information can’t be more than 6 hours older than the Ebay site, and other content no more than 24 hours older. If it’s older, you must disclose how much.   
* You must delete content from your app once it’s no longer publicly available on Ebay.  
* You can’t store Ebay content except as the terms allow, and after termination you must destroy copies within 10 days. 


	ToS: display/attribution

* Public display of Ebay content can’t be combined with non-ebay content, and it must be visually isolated. May conflict with retailer comparison.  
* The agreement bars using Ebay content, alone or with third-party data, to suggest or model prices for items listed on Ebay sites  
* The program is meant for promoting and facilitating access to Ebay’s services, and Ebay may restrict access if it believes you undermine its business interests. 

* [Best Buy API](https://bestbuyapis.github.io/api-documentation/)  
  Type: retailer API

	Free tier: 

* Hard limits are 5 requests per second and 50,000 per day per key. Applies to the Products, Reviews, Stores, Categories, Recommendations, and buying options APIs. Going over returns a 403 error and an email to the key holder.   
* There’s no charge per request within the quota  
* Signup: An API key from the developer portal. 

	[ToS](https://developer.bestbuy.com/legal):  
	ToS: storing/caching

* 72 hour limit: The Prohibited Uses section bars storing or caching any content except temporarily, for no more than 72 hours, and only as needed to speed up displaying it. That conflicts with long-term price history.   
* No copying or redistribution: Unless another agreement allows it, you can’t reproduce, modify, sell, distribute, download, transmit, or create derivative works of the service or the content

	ToS: display/attribution

* Attribution is required: You must clearly and conspicuously attribute the source of all content as received from Best Buy  
* Content must stay intact: You cannot modify or hide the links to Best Buy’s website, or alter the text, images, logos, or copyright notices. You can resize images if you keep the proportions.   
* Logo required: The branding guidelines say every page, screen, or widget where a Best Buy API appears must show the Best Buy logo, placed where the data appears. It can’t be larger than your own branding or the most prominent element.   
* Naming and look: Don’t use “Best Buy” in your app’s name without written pre-approval, and don’t copy Best Buy’s blue-and-yellow look.   
* Publicity: Best Buy gets a perpetual, irrevocable license to use your name, logo, and screenshots to advertise your use of the API.
